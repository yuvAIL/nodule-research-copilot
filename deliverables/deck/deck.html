What this example shows

The volume moved toward the expert's (43.8 → 46.8 of 55.0 mL), and the change is tracked. One stroke at the left edge overshot the long axis (65.1 vs 59.8 mm). Undo is one key away, and correcting 5 of about 40 slices closed only part of the gap.
The strokes were made by our test script, not by a researcher, so this example tells us nothing about correction time.

Matching status

↔ ▼45% vs Baseline? suggestedconfirmed by researcher (A / M)
Every follow-up finding is paired automatically with the nearest earlier finding (one-to-one, earlier scans only, ≤ 50 mm). Here the distance was 38 mm. The "?" stays until the researcher confirms the pair.

Read this change with care

• The two scans were reconstructed differently (STANDARD vs LUNG kernel), which by itself changes measured volume.
• The baseline has no expert outline. On the follow-up, the AI volume is 20 % below the expert's.
• Two scans of the same day differ by a median 9.5 % (our click) to 17 % (expert) in volume, over 28 patients.
→ The app computes the change. Whether the tumour really shrank is the researcher's call.
It saves the re-find and the side-by-side arithmetic, about 10 min per follow-up by Daniel's estimate.

Limits of this evidence

• RIDER (59 scans) is mostly large solid tumours; LIDC adds 35 nodules on 15 scans, only 8 of them sub-solid
• False positives: only an upper bound (LIDC: median 1 per scan); not scorable on RIDER
• No researcher has been timed yet, and "manual" is Daniel's own estimate
• AI timing comes from one laptop. App timing comes from a slow test machine
